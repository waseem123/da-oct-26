
Task 3: Filtering & Slicing High-Value Transactions

• Objective: Isolate your inventory distribution metrics specifically for transactions that went through customer membership accounts.
• Layout Mapping:
	• Drag Product_Name into the Rows quadrant.
	• Drag Sales_Amount into the Values quadrant (Set to Sum).
	• Drag Customer_Type into the Filters quadrant.
• The Slicer Feature: Go to the PivotTable Analyze tab on the top ribbon, click Insert Slicer, and check the box for Region. Now use the floating slicer buttons to check how sales shift when jumping between North and West.

Task 4: Advanced Value Representation (% of Grand Total)

• Objective: Instead of reading pure currency numbers, convert your PivotTable to show what percentage each product category contributes to your business.
• Layout Mapping:
	• Drag Category into the Rows quadrant.
	• Drag Sales_Amount into the Values quadrant.
• The Trick: Right-click any calculation cell inside the table, go to Show Values As, and choose % of Grand Total.
